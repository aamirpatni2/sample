Our Menu

Signature dishes worth the craving.

A menu built around the flavors of Lahore's food street — grilled, slow-cooked, and served fresh daily.

Sizzling BBQ Platters

Seekh kebab, chicken tikka, and malai boti grilled fresh over charcoal — smoky, juicy, and full of flavor.

Signature Karahi

Our most-ordered dish — chicken or mutton karahi cooked in a rich tomato-based masala, made fresh per order.

Slow-Cooked Nihari

Simmered overnight for 8+ hours until the meat falls off the bone — a Lahori breakfast classic, served all day.

Biryani & Pulao

Fresh Naan & Roti

Baked to order in our clay tandoor every few minutes, so it reaches your table warm and never stale.

Desserts & Kulfi
